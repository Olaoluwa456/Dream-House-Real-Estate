Privacy Notice

1. Who is responsible?

The actual business operating this website must identify itself as the data controller, publish real contact details and explain how visitors can submit a privacy request. “Dream House Real Estate” is a sample brand in this demonstration; no real privacy team or compliance certification is represented.

2. Information that may be collected

A real version of this site may receive a visitor's name, email, phone number, enquiry category, preferred location, message and property interests. Basic technical data may be processed by the hosting provider. This demo uses browser local storage to remember saved properties, cookie choices and form-test submissions. No analytics or advertising trackers are installed in this package.

3. Purposes and lawful basis

Personal information should be collected only for identified and legitimate purposes, such as responding to a request, arranging a service, keeping appropriate records, preventing abuse or meeting a legal obligation. The controller must determine and document the lawful basis for each activity under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable rules. Where consent is relied on, it should be informed, specific and freely given and should not be pre-selected.

4. Sharing, security and retention

A live site may need to share relevant details with authorised staff, professional advisers, service providers or public authorities where legally required. It should use appropriate security safeguards, limit access, avoid unnecessary collection and retain personal information only for an appropriate, documented period. The local demo data can be removed by clearing this browser's site storage.

5. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of processing, individuals may have rights to information about processing, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability and withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis. The real operator should describe how to submit requests and how to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Response times, exemptions and applicable rights should be confirmed with qualified counsel.

6. Cookies and local storage

This demo uses essential browser storage for interface preferences and demonstration features. It does not load analytics or advertising scripts. A production site should accurately disclose every cookie, pixel, embedded tool and third-party integration and provide controls where required. Changing a preference here stores a local preference only; it does not control third-party websites.
